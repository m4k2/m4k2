## About me

I started learning blockchain security in 2022. Before Zellic, I researched privacy and obfuscation in decentralized systems for the **French Government**
(2023–2024), including a multi-layered privacy circuit built on decentralized technologies.

At **Crédit Mutuel Arkéa's CERT/SIRT** (2021–2023), I worked on threat intelligence, built a crisis-management tool, and set up a forensic laboratory.

My degrees are in computer science and electronics, including an M.S. in Computer Science at ENSIBS, a B.S. in Software Engineering, and a B.S. in Industrial
Electronics and Computer Engineering.

I was a **firefighter from 2016 to 2023**. That included rescue operations, road rescues, and urban and forest fires.
